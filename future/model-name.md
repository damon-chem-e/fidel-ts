The Lynx model series is named for the wild cat known for its visual acuity and patient hunting strategy. Just as the Lynx uses its exceptional eyesight to spot subtle movements across a vast distance and its tufted ears to process faint sounds, the model is designed to observe and synthesize context from across various data modalities (text, vision, time series, etc.). The model embodies the Lynx's patience and stealth: through gating structure, it selectively ignores irrelevant data streams, waiting until the moment the multimodal context becomes salient. The Lynx lives in deep forests or mountains, and our models are designed to operate in similarly complex and heterogeneous environments.
